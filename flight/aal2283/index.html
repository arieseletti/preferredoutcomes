What do I do if my flight is delayed?

You should contact the airline for updates and inquire about compensation options.

How much compensation can I receive?

You may be entitled to up to $2000 depending on the circumstances of the delay.

What rights do I have as a passenger?

Passengers have the right to compensation for significant delays and should be informed of their options.
