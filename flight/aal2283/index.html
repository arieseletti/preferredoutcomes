What should I do during a delay?

During a delay, it's advisable to keep in touch with the airline for updates, stay near the gate for boarding notices, and consider your options for food and rest if the delay is lengthy.

Will I be compensated for the delay?

According to US regulations, compensation may not be guaranteed for all delays. However, if the delay was caused by the airline's actions, you may be eligible for compensation. It's best to contact the airline for specific information.

How can I check the status of my flight?

You can check the status of your flight by visiting the airline's website or by using flight tracking apps that provide real-time updates.
