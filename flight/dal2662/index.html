What are my rights for flight delays?

Passengers have rights under U.S. Department of Transportation regulations which state that you may be entitled to compensation for significant delays.

How much can I claim for a delayed flight?

You may be eligible to claim up to USD 700 depending on the length of your delay and other factors.

How do I file a claim?

You can file a claim directly with the airline, providing details about your flight and the delay.
