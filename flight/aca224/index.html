What are my rights as a passenger for compensation?

As a passenger, your rights to compensation depend on the regulations applicable in your region and the circumstances of the delay. Generally, if your flight is delayed for more than three hours and the delay is within the airline's control, you may be entitled to compensation. It's important to check the specific airline policies and relevant aviation laws to understand your rights fully.

How do I file a compensation claim?

To file a compensation claim, you should first gather all necessary documentation, including your boarding pass, receipts for any expenses incurred due to the delay, and any communication from the airline regarding the delay. Next, visit the airline's official website to find their claims process, which may include filling out an online form or sending a written request. Be sure to submit your claim within the specified time frame set by the airline to ensure it is considered.

What if my claim is denied?

If your compensation claim is denied, you have several options to consider. You can request a detailed explanation from the airline regarding the denial and review their policies to ensure your claim was valid. If you still believe you are entitled to compensation, you may escalate the matter to a consumer protection agency or seek legal advice to explore further options for resolution.
