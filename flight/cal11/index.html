What are my rights regarding flight delays?

Under U.S. Department of Transportation regulations, passengers have specific rights when their flights are delayed. If a flight is delayed for more than three hours, passengers may be entitled to compensation, which can include refunds or travel vouchers. It is important to check the airline's specific policies, as they can vary significantly.

How can I file a compensation claim?

To file a compensation claim for a delayed flight, passengers should first gather all relevant documentation, including boarding passes and any notifications from the airline. Claims can often be submitted directly through the airline's website or customer service channels. Additionally, there are third-party services that specialize in assisting passengers with compensation claims, which can simplify the process.

What compensation can I expect for a delay?

Compensation for flight delays can vary depending on the airline's policies and the length of the delay.
