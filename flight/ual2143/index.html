What are my rights for a delayed flight?

Under US regulations, if your flight is significantly delayed, you may be entitled to compensation depending on the airline's policies.

How can I claim compensation?

You can claim compensation by contacting the airline's customer service or by using third-party compensation services.

What determines the amount of compensation?

Compensation amounts can depend on factors like the length of the delay, the distance of the flight, and the airline's policies.
