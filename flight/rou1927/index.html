Know Your Rights: Flight Delay Compensation

If your flight is delayed, you may be entitled to compensation. Check for your eligibility and understand your rights.
// MAX: ()● LIVE

What causes flight delays?

Flight delays can be caused by various factors including weather conditions, air traffic control issues, technical problems with aircraft, or operational challenges.

How can I check the status of my flight?

You can check the status of your flight on the airline’s website, through their mobile app, or by calling their customer service.

What should I do if my flight is delayed?

If your flight is delayed, stay updated through the airline, consider your compensation options, and keep your receipts for potential reimbursements.
