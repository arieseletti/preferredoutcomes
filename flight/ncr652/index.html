What are my rights for compensation after a flight delay?

Passengers have specific rights when it comes to flight delays, particularly under regulations set by the U.S. Department of Transportation. If a flight is delayed for more than three hours, travelers may be entitled to compensation, which can include refunds or travel vouchers. It is important to check the airline's policy regarding delays, as some airlines offer additional compensation for significant disruptions. Additionally, documenting all communications and flight details can strengthen your claim for compensation.

How do I file a claim for a delayed flight?

Filing a claim for a delayed flight involves several steps to ensure that your request is processed efficiently. First, gather all necessary documentation, including your flight number, date of travel, and any receipts related to expenses incurred due to the delay. Next, visit the airline's official website to locate their claims process, which often includes a specific form to fill out. Submitting your claim promptly is crucial, as airlines may have time limits for processing compensation requests.

What should I do if my airline denies my compensation claim?

If your airline denies your compensation claim, it is essential to understand your options for recourse. Start by reviewing the airline's response to ensure you comprehend their reasoning for the denial. If you believe your claim is valid, you can escalate the matter by contacting the U.S. Department of Transportation to file a complaint. Additionally, consider seeking assistance from consumer advocacy groups that specialize in air travel issues, as they can provide guidance on how to pursue your claim further.
