What are my rights as a passenger for a delayed flight?

As a passenger, you have specific rights when your flight is delayed, especially if the delay exceeds a certain threshold. Airlines are typically required to provide compensation, which may include refunds or additional services such as meal vouchers or hotel accommodations, depending on the length of the delay and the airline's policies. It's crucial to familiarize yourself with the airline's compensation policy and any applicable regulations to ensure you receive the compensation you are entitled to.

How can I claim compensation for my delayed flight?

To claim compensation for a delayed flight, start by gathering all relevant documentation, including your boarding pass, receipts for any expenses incurred, and any communication you had with the airline. Next, submit a formal complaint or claim through the airline's customer service channels, clearly stating your case and the compensation you are seeking. If the airline does not respond satisfactorily, you can escalate the issue to consumer protection agencies or seek legal assistance to pursue your claim further.
