What's happening with EV federal support?

EV manufacturers are adjusting their expectations as they no longer anticipate federal buyer credits to return after the recent midterm elections.

How does this affect EV owners?

Current and potential EV owners may face a lack of financial incentives, making EV purchases less affordable and impacting the overall market for electric vehicles.

What can consumers do?

Consumers should stay informed about changes in federal policy and explore potential refunds or payouts to compensate for the loss of expected incentives.
