Are You Affected by EV Subsidy Changes?

With federal EV subsidies uncertain after the recent midterms, many consumers are left worrying about the future of electric vehicle affordability.
Join thousands of consumers taking action!● LIVE

What should I be concerned about regarding EV subsidies?

Many electric vehicle manufacturers, including Rivian, Lucid, and Scout, are not expecting the reinstatement of federal subsidies aimed at reducing purchase costs for consumers. This could lead to increased prices for EVs in the near future.

How can I ensure I'm protected?

Stay informed about legislative changes affecting EV subsidies and consider consulting with consumer advocacy groups to share your concerns and weigh your options.

What actions can I take if I feel misled?

You can participate in petitions to advocate for the restoration of EV subsidies, which may help ensure that consumer interests are represented.
