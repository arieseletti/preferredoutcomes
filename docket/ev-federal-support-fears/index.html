What is happening with EV federal support?

Recent political shifts have left EV manufacturers uncertain about the restoration of federal buyer credits, impacting consumer expectations.

How does this affect EV buyers?

Many prospective EV buyers may hesitate to make a purchase without the financial incentives that previously made electric vehicles more affordable.

What can I do about it?

Stay informed and voice your concerns. Sign the petition for a clear policy that supports electric vehicle buyers and incentivizes sustainable choices.
