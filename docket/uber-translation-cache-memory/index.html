This reduction is particularly noteworthy as it comes with a commitment to keep the P99 latency below 1 millisecond, which is essential for maintaining a smooth user experience. However, such drastic changes raise questions about the potential impact on translation accuracy and reliability for users who depend on the service.

While Uber touts the benefits of increased efficiency, the implications of reduced cache memory could lead to challenges in translation quality. Users may experience delays or inaccuracies in translations, especially in high-demand scenarios where immediate responses are critical. The balance between efficiency and quality is delicate, and stakeholders must remain vigilant as these changes take effect. Monitoring user feedback and performance metrics will be essential in assessing the real-world impact of this significant reduction.

Uber is ensuring low latency by implementing advanced optimization techniques that allow them to maintain a P99 latency of below 1 millisecond despite the significant reduction in translation cache memory.
