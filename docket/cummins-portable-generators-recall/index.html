Cummins Recalls Portable Generators for Safety Concerns

Direct Summary: Cummins has issued a recall for nearly 3,000 portable generators due to safety concerns involving fuel spraying and caps ejecting.
DOCKET LEDGER: CUMMINS-GENERATOR-RECALL (31 OF 50 BATCH SLOTS FILLED) ● ACTIVE INTAKE

What should I do if I own one of the recalled generators?

If you own one of the recalled generators, stop using it immediately and follow the recall instructions provided by Cummins.

How can I find out if my generator is affected?

You can check the Cummins website or contact their customer service for information on whether your generator is part of the recall.

What are the risks associated with using the recalled generators?

Using the recalled generators poses risks of fuel spraying, which can lead to fire hazards and potential injuries.
