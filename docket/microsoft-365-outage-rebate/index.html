Why do companies shortchange victims with standard responses or class actions?

Companies often use boilerplate denials or generic responses to avoid accountability, leaving consumers with little recourse. In many cases, they distract affected users with offers of free credit monitoring or lengthy class action settlements that can take years to resolve, during which time lawyers often take a hefty cut of any awarded damages. This strategy minimizes their liability while leaving consumers frustrated and without immediate support. It's essential to recognize these tactics and pursue direct action to secure your rightful compensation.
