Why do typical company responses or class actions often fail to fully support victims?

Often, companies respond to data breaches with boilerplate denials and offer superficial solutions like free credit monitoring, which fail to address the financial and emotional toll on victims. Class action settlements can be lengthy and convoluted, with legal fees consuming a significant portion of the payout—sometimes as much as 40%. This leaves affected individuals with only a fraction of what they deserve, while the companies dodge accountability and continue to profit. It’s essential for victims to understand their rights and seek proper compensation.
