What are gamers saying about GTA 6?

Gamers are expressing a wide range of reactions to the release of Grand Theft Auto 6 in New Zealand. This mix of enthusiasm and skepticism reflects the high expectations that come with a franchise as iconic as GTA.

What criticisms have been raised about the game?

Critics of Grand Theft Auto 6 have pointed out concerns regarding the graphics and gameplay mechanics, suggesting that they may not live up to the standards set by previous titles in the series. Some gamers feel that the visuals lack the innovation expected from a new release, while others have noted gameplay elements that seem outdated or unrefined. This feedback indicates a divide among the community, with some players eagerly embracing the new installment while others remain cautious and critical of its execution.
