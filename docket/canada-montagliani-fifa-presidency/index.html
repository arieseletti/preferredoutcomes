What does Montagliani's absence mean for FIFA?

Montagliani's absence from the FIFA presidential race signifies a potential shift in leadership dynamics within the organization. Without his candidacy, the race may see new contenders who could either continue the current trajectory or propose significant changes to how FIFA operates.
