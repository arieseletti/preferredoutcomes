What caused the Dow's significant drop today?

This news triggered a sell-off in tech stocks, leading to a broader market decline. As Nvidia is a major player in the tech sector, its performance can heavily influence market sentiment and investor confidence.

How are investors reacting to the market fluctuations?

Investors are reacting to the market fluctuations with a mix of caution and opportunism. Some are concerned about the implications of Nvidia's performance on the tech sector and are opting to hold off on making any significant moves.
