What should I do during these weather alerts?

Stay indoors if possible, avoid unnecessary travel, and ensure that you have essential supplies. Keep an eye on local news for updates.

How can I stay safe in icy conditions?

Wear appropriate footwear, be cautious when walking outside, and report any hazardous conditions to local authorities.

Is there assistance available for vulnerable individuals during these alerts?

Yes, many local councils offer support for vulnerable individuals during cold weather. Contact your local council for information on available services.
