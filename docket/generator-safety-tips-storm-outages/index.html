What are the key safety tips for using a generator during a storm?

Key safety tips include keeping the generator outdoors, using heavy-duty extension cords, and never connecting it directly to your home's wiring.

How can I ensure my generator is safe to use?

Regular maintenance, checking for fuel leaks, and ensuring proper ventilation are essential for safe generator use.

What should I do if my generator is recalled?

If your generator is recalled, stop using it immediately and follow the manufacturer's instructions for returns or repairs.
