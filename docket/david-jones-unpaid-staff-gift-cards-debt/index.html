What should employees do about unpaid wages from David Jones?

Employees of David Jones who are facing unpaid wages should first document their hours worked and any communications regarding their pay. They can then reach out to the Fair Work Ombudsman, which is responsible for enforcing workplace rights in Australia, to lodge a formal complaint. It's essential to provide evidence of the unpaid wages, as this will strengthen their case. Additionally, employees may consider joining forces with colleagues to collectively address the issue, which can increase pressure on the company to resolve the matter promptly.

What are the rights of consumers with unused gift cards?

Consumers holding unused gift cards from David Jones should be aware of their rights, particularly in light of the company's financial difficulties. Under Australian consumer law, gift cards must have a minimum expiry period of three years, and consumers should not lose their rights to redeem these cards due to the company's financial issues. It is advisable for consumers to monitor the situation closely and stay informed about any changes to the company's policies regarding gift cards. If necessary, they can also contact consumer protection agencies for guidance on how to proceed and ensure their rights are upheld.

How can consumers take action against David Jones?

Organizing or participating in petitions can also be an effective way to demonstrate collective dissatisfaction and push for accountability. Lastly, staying informed about any legal proceedings or class actions that may arise can provide further avenues for recourse.
