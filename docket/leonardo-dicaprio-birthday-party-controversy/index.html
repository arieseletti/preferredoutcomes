How has social media reacted to the party?

Social media has reacted with a mix of outrage and support regarding the party, with many users expressing their opinions on platforms like Twitter and Instagram. Some individuals have criticized DiCaprio for the perceived inappropriateness of the guest list, while others have defended him, arguing that he is simply celebrating with friends. This divide has sparked a lively debate, with hashtags and discussions trending as fans and critics alike weigh in on the situation.

This incident reveals much about celebrity culture, particularly how public figures are scrutinized for their personal lives and choices. The focus on DiCaprio's birthday party highlights societal expectations regarding relationships, especially concerning age differences.
